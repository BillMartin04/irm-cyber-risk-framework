## Read

The Board, the CISO, the CIO, and the Audit Committee are not interchangeable. In a well-run enterprise, each of them makes a different class of decision, on a different clock, with a different accountability. The Board owns **strategic risk appetite** and long-run capital allocation. The Audit Committee owns **independent assurance** — including approving the internal audit plan and hearing findings without management filtering. The CIO owns **operational technology delivery and first-line technology controls**. The CISO owns **second-line cyber-risk oversight** and, in most modern operating models, is escalated to reporting into the CEO or CRO rather than the CIO to preserve independence.

The friction between these roles is not a bug — it is how independent judgement is preserved. A GRC professional who understands the friction can navigate it. One who tries to smooth it out usually ends up neutralising the very oversight the structure was designed to create.



## Apply

- Map your own organisation's four roles. If any two collapse into one person, name the specific risk that creates.

## Read

A GRC Charter is a short, board-ratified document that gives the GRC function its mandate: its scope, its authority, its reporting lines, and the specific decisions it is empowered to make. Without a charter, GRC teams end up doing whatever the loudest stakeholder asked for last week. With a charter, they can refuse work that is out of scope and demand access to work that is in scope.

The charter is complemented by a **Stakeholder Engagement Plan** — a living map of who needs to be consulted, informed, or approved for each recurring GRC activity. A weak engagement plan is why audit findings arrive as surprises. A strong one is why the CISO is never blindsided at a board meeting.



## Apply

- Draft a one-page GRC Charter for your organisation. Include: scope, authority, exclusions, reporting lines.
- Build a stakeholder matrix (RACI) for the next quarterly risk review.

## Done When

- You have a charter draft you would be willing to defend to a CFO.

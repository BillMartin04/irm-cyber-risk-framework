## Read

The internal GRC analyst and the external strategic advisor are not the same job, and pretending they are is one of the reasons early-career GRC professionals stall. The **internal analyst** lives inside the operating model: they own recurring evidence collection, they maintain the risk register, they file exceptions, they are on the hook when an auditor calls. The **external strategic advisor** is invited in for a specific outcome — a maturity assessment, a regulatory response plan, an M&A due-diligence review — and their leverage is the outside perspective, the frameworks they carry between clients, and the willingness to challenge the status quo without political cost.

Both roles need the same technical foundation, but they optimise for different things. The analyst optimises for defensibility and repeatability. The advisor optimises for insight and executive access. If you plan to move from one to the other — as most course graduates do — you need to be deliberate about which muscle you are building at any moment.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

Internal analysts spend most of their day inside a ServiceNow IRM instance operating standard queues: Risks pending review, Assessments due, Issues open past SLA, Policy exceptions expiring. Their job is to keep those queues green.

Advisors show up with playbooks that shape the instance itself: control library design, authority document ingestion strategy, assessment cadence, KRI thresholds. If you want to become an advisor, learn to design the workspace, not just work inside it.

## Live in Action — Lumina Cyber Risk (Open Source)

Lumina is deliberately small enough for a solo advisor to fork, customise, and demo to a client. Treat it as your portable maturity-model demo when you are pitching an advisory engagement.

## Apply

- Draft a one-paragraph biography for yourself as an internal analyst and a separate paragraph as an external advisor. Notice which one feels more true today.
- Pick one advisory-side skill (framework mapping, board reporting, quantification) and give yourself 30 days to build a deliverable that proves it.

## Done When

- You can articulate the operating differences between the two roles in less than sixty seconds.

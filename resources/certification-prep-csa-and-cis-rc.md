This course is not an exam-cram, but its content maps directly to the following certifications. Use the mapping to focus your revision.

## ServiceNow certifications

- **CSA (Certified System Administrator)** — foundational platform knowledge. Use the [ServiceNow CSA Course](https://github.com/BillMartin04/servicenow-csa-course) alongside this one.
- **CIS-Risk & Compliance (CIS-RC)** — ServiceNow IRM specialist. Modules 2, 3, 4, 12, 15 of this course map directly.
- **CIS-Vendor Risk Management (CIS-VRM)** — Module 9 maps directly.

## GRC certifications

- **CRISC (ISACA)** — risk identification, assessment, response, and monitoring. Modules 1, 2, 3, 10, 15 map.
- **CISA (ISACA)** — auditing. Module 15 is the direct match, plus Modules 3 and 18.
- **CISM (ISACA)** — information security management. Modules 1, 3, 8, 17.

## ISO certifications

- **ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer** — [Module 18](../module-18-iso-27001-lead-implementation/README.md) is the masterclass.
- **ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor** — Module 18 plus Module 15.

## FAIR

- **Open FAIR Foundation** — [Module 10](../module-10-cyber-risk-quantification-fair/README.md) provides the framework foundation.

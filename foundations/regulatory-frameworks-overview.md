The regulatory landscape is dense and moving fast. This page names the frameworks and regulations you will encounter in the course, grouped by function. Each is treated in depth in the module that owns it.

## Framework backbones

- **NIST CSF 2.0** — the universally applicable cyber-security framework, with an elevated *Govern* function. See [Module 3.1](../module-03-frameworks-and-baselines/lesson-3-1.md).
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** — the certification-grade Information Security Management System standard. See [Module 3.2](../module-03-frameworks-and-baselines/lesson-3-2.md) and [Module 18](../module-18-iso-27001-lead-implementation/README.md).
- **CIS Controls v8** — prioritised, prescriptive controls for resource-constrained programs. See [Module 3.3](../module-03-frameworks-and-baselines/lesson-3-3.md).

## Industry baselines

- **PCI DSS v4.0** — payment card processing.
- **HIPAA Security Rule** — US healthcare.
- **SOC 2 Type II** — SaaS trust-services attestation.

## Modern regulations you must know

- **EU DORA** — financial-sector operational resilience. See [Module 14.1](../module-14-regulatory-resilience/lesson-14-1.md).
- **SEC Cyber Disclosure (Form 8-K 4-Day Rule)** — public-company mandatory disclosure. See [Module 14.2](../module-14-regulatory-resilience/lesson-14-2.md).
- **NIS2 Directive** — EU-wide critical-sector cyber-security obligations with personal executive liability. See [Module 14.3](../module-14-regulatory-resilience/lesson-14-3.md).
- **EU AI Act** — risk-tiered AI regulation. See [Module 11.4](../module-11-ai-governance/lesson-11-4.md).
- **GDPR / CCPA** — data privacy regulation. See [Module 6.5](../module-06-data-protection-and-privacy/lesson-6-5.md).

## AI-specific frames

- **NIST AI RMF** and **ISO/IEC 42001** — the two dominant AI risk-management frames. See [Module 11.2](../module-11-ai-governance/lesson-11-2.md).
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLMs** — the practitioner-grade LLM threat catalogue. See [Module 11.3](../module-11-ai-governance/lesson-11-3.md).

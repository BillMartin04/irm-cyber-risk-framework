Traditional GRC tools (Archer, MetricStream, older module-based platforms) treat risk, compliance, and audit as separate applications, each with its own data model, workflow, and reporting. **Integrated Risk Management (IRM)** platforms — ServiceNow IRM being the reference example — treat them as one linked graph on top of a shared CMDB and workflow engine.

## Why the shift matters

- **One CMDB.** A risk, a control, and an audit finding all point at the same Business Application. Aggregation writes itself.
- **One workflow engine.** Assessments, issues, and exceptions all use the same task engine, the same SLAs, and the same notifications.
- **One reporting layer.** Board dashboards pull from the same data that first-line operators use.

## What that unlocks

- **Continuous Controls Monitoring** (Module 12) becomes feasible because control tests, evidence, and KRIs live on the same platform.
- **AI-assisted risk sensing** becomes meaningful because the graph is rich enough to feed models.
- **Executive dashboards** can be trusted because they are built on the same records the first-line owners are still updating.

This is why the course is aligned to ServiceNow IRM and demonstrated in miniature on the Lumina Cyber Risk portal — both are IRM patterns, not legacy GRC patterns.

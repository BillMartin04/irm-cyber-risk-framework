# GRC Terminology Glossary

- **Appetite** — the amount of risk the board is willing to accept in pursuit of strategy.
- **Assessment** — a recurring evidence-collection pass against a risk, control, or authority document.
- **Authority Document** — an external framework or regulation citated by internal controls (e.g., NIST CSF, ISO 27001, PCI DSS).
- **CI (Configuration Item)** — an atomic asset record in the CMDB.
- **CMDB** — Configuration Management Database.
- **Control** — a mechanism that reduces the likelihood or impact of a risk.
- **Control Objective** — the outcome a control is designed to produce.
- **CSDM** — Common Service Data Model, ServiceNow's endorsed CMDB schema.
- **Exception** — a formal, time-limited acceptance of a policy or control deviation.
- **FAIR** — Factor Analysis of Information Risk, a quantitative risk model. See [Module 10](../module-10-cyber-risk-quantification-fair/README.md).
- **Inherent Risk** — the level of risk before controls are applied.
- **IRM** — Integrated Risk Management platform architecture.
- **Issue** — a remediation task discharging a risk treatment or audit finding.
- **KRI / KPI** — Key Risk / Performance Indicator; monitored metrics with thresholds.
- **Residual Risk** — the level of risk after controls are applied.
- **Risk Register** — the enduring catalogue of identified risks.
- **SoA** — Statement of Applicability, an ISO 27001 artefact listing which Annex A controls apply.
- **SoD** — Segregation of Duties, an IAM control preventing conflicting entitlements on one identity.
- **TOD / TOE** — Test of Design / Test of Operating Effectiveness, audit testing types.
- **Threshold** — the KRI value at which action is triggered.
- **Tolerance** — the acceptable variation around a KRI.
- **UCF** — Unified Control Framework.

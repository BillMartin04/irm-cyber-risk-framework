# Module 17: Executive Advisory

{% hint style="success" %}
**4LOD Alignment:** 4th Line (Board Oversight & Strategic Leadership)
**Primary Persona:** CISOs, Strategic Business Advisors, Management Consultants
{% endhint %}

## What this module covers

**Executive Advisory, Board Reporting, and Strategy Execution.** By the end of this module you will have added a specific, portable capability to your GRC practice — one that you can demonstrate on an interview, deploy in a client engagement, or promote inside your own organisation.

## Key takeaways

- Communicating effectively with board members and CEOs.
- Positioning security as a business enabler.
- Winning executive funding for major initiatives.

## Module deliverable

> Create an Executive Board Presentation Deck summarising enterprise risk posture, regulatory readiness, and budget requests.

Every lesson in this module builds toward that deliverable. Complete every lesson before submitting the deliverable.

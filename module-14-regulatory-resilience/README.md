## What this module covers

**Regulatory Resilience and Mandatory Incident Disclosures.** By the end of this module you will have added a specific, portable capability to your GRC practice — one that you can demonstrate on an interview, deploy in a client engagement, or promote inside your own organisation.

## Key takeaways

- Navigating aggressive mandatory notification timelines.
- Preparing executive leadership for statutory personal liability under modern directives.

## Module deliverable

> Construct a 72-Hour Regulatory Breach Notification Playbook aligned with global disclosure mandates.

Every lesson in this module builds toward that deliverable. Complete every lesson before submitting the deliverable.

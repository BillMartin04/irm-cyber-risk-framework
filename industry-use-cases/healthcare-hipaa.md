# Healthcare (HIPAA)

Healthcare organisations govern **Protected Health Information (PHI)** under HIPAA in the US and equivalent statutes globally. The cyber-risk profile is dominated by ransomware exposure, medical-device (IoMT) sprawl, and workforce mobility.

## Priority modules for healthcare practitioners

- **Module 3.4** — HIPAA Security Rule baselines.
- **Module 4** — CMDB & Asset Governance, including medical devices as first-class CIs.
- **Module 6** — Data Protection & Privacy.
- **Module 8** — Incident Response, especially ransomware tabletop exercises.
- **Module 14** — Regulatory Resilience, including HHS OCR breach notification.

## Common failure modes

- CMDB coverage that stops at IT assets and omits medical devices.
- BIA scoring that under-weights patient-care continuity.
- Breach-notification playbooks that assume a single US federal authority instead of state-by-state overlay.

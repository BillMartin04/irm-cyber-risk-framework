# Banking and Financial Services

Banking and financial services (BFS) is the most demanding cyber-risk environment in most enterprise portfolios: multi-jurisdictional regulation, aggressive breach-notification timelines, systemic third-party risk, and executive personal liability under recent directives.

## Priority modules for BFS practitioners

- **Module 3** — Frameworks & Baselines. PCI DSS v4.0 and SOC 2 are baseline requirements.
- **Module 9** — Third-Party Risk. Concentration risk in cloud and payments is a systemic exposure.
- **Module 10** — FAIR Quantification. BFS boards want financial ranges, not heat maps.
- **Module 14** — Regulatory Resilience. DORA, NIS2, and SEC 8-K disclosure obligations converge here.
- **Module 15** — Audit Execution. BFS lives under continuous regulatory examination.

## Common failure modes

- Treating DORA as an IT-ops project rather than a Board-level operational-resilience commitment.
- Under-investing in Fourth-Party (n-tier vendor) risk visibility.
- Running qualitative risk registers on multi-billion-dollar exposures.

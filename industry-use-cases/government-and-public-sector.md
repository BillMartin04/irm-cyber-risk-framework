# Government and Public Sector

Public-sector cyber-risk lives under statutory frameworks (FISMA / FedRAMP in the US, national schemes elsewhere), sustained supply-chain scrutiny, and public disclosure obligations that magnify reputational impact.

## Priority modules

- **Module 3** — Frameworks & Baselines, including FISMA / FedRAMP mapping to NIST 800-53.
- **Module 9** — Third-Party Risk. Software supply-chain executive orders now demand SBOM and attested pipelines.
- **Module 11** — AI Governance. Public-sector AI use is under intensifying legislative scrutiny.
- **Module 15** — Audit Execution, including Inspector General audits.
- **Module 17** — Executive Advisory, especially communicating with elected officials.

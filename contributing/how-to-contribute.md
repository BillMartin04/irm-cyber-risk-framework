This course is open source. Contributions are welcome — corrections, additions, translations, and lesson improvements.

## The workflow

1. Open the [course repo](https://github.com/BillMartin04/irm-cyber-risk-framework) on GitHub.
2. Fork it into your own account.
3. Create a branch for your change (`fix/module-3-typo`, `add/module-10-example`, etc.).
4. Commit your changes, push, and open a pull request against `master`.
5. Wait for review. Small fixes typically merge within days.

## What to work on

- Fix typos, broken links, or unclear phrasing.
- Add worked examples in the **Apply** section of any lesson.
- Contribute case studies for **Industry Use Cases**.
- Extend the **Lumina Cyber Risk** repo — see its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Read

Every identified risk gets one of four treatment decisions: **Accept**, **Mitigate**, **Transfer**, or **Avoid**. Accept means the residual risk is inside appetite and no further action is required. Mitigate means new or strengthened controls will be applied. Transfer means the exposure is shifted contractually or through insurance. Avoid means the underlying activity is stopped. The choice is a governance decision, not a technical one — it needs the right owner at the right line of defense to make it.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

Treatment decisions in ServiceNow IRM belong on the Risk record itself, not on an Issue. Issues are the remediation tasks that discharge a mitigation decision. Confusing the two produces registers where the treatment history is unreadable.


## Apply

- Pick five risks and decide which of the four treatments applies. Name the human who is authorised to make each call.

## Done When

- Every risk in your top-ten register has an explicit, named treatment decision.

## Read

Risk exceptions are formal, time-limited acceptances of policy deviation. A healthy exception workflow requires: a documented business justification, a named accepting authority (typically at or above the risk owner), compensating controls, an expiry date, and a re-review trigger. Exceptions that never expire are the single most common finding in a mature audit.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

Use the Policy Exception module in ServiceNow IRM with mandatory expiry dates and automated notifications to the accepting authority at 30 / 15 / 5 days out. Never allow a policy exception without a compensating control field.


## Apply

- Audit your existing exceptions. Any without an expiry date should be reset with one this quarter.

## Done When

- 100% of open exceptions in your register have an expiry date and a compensating control.

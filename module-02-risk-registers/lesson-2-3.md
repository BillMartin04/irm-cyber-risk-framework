## Read

Risk appetite is the amount of risk the board is willing to accept in pursuit of strategy. Risk tolerance is the acceptable variation around a specific KRI. Threshold is the number at which action is triggered. These are three distinct levers and each has a different audience: appetite is a board conversation, tolerance is a committee conversation, threshold is an operator conversation.

A well-designed GRC program can point to a specific appetite statement, cascade it down into tolerance ranges per risk category, and further down into automated thresholds on KRIs monitored inside the IRM platform.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

In ServiceNow IRM, appetite lives on Risk Frameworks (or Policy records depending on your version), tolerance ranges live on Risk Categories, and thresholds live on Indicators / KRIs. Design the cascade explicitly rather than embedding numbers in prose.


## Apply

- Write your board's cyber risk appetite in a single, testable sentence.

## Done When

- You have appetite, tolerance, and threshold expressed as three distinct artefacts.

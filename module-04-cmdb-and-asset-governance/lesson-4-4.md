## Read

Shadow IT is not eliminated by a policy. It is triaged by a workflow. Every mature organisation has unauthorised SaaS, unauthorised cloud accounts, and unauthorised AI tools in use right now. The GRC objective is to detect them, assess them, and either sanction them (bring under governance) or retire them.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

ServiceNow SaaS Management, combined with financial system integration (expense reports catch unauthorised SaaS spend), gives you the detection layer. IRM Assessment templates give you the sanctioning workflow.


## Apply

- Run an expense-report scan for SaaS keywords. You will find shadow IT you did not know existed.

## Done When

- You have a documented sanction / retire decision for at least five instances of shadow IT.

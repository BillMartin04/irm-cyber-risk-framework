## Read

Asset discovery is the foundation of every downstream cyber-risk calculation. If you cannot enumerate what you own, you cannot defend it. Modern discovery spans hardware (endpoints, servers, network devices), software (installed applications, SaaS subscriptions), cloud resources (compute, storage, IAM identities), and data stores (databases, buckets, warehouses). Each surface uses different tooling and each has different failure modes.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

ServiceNow Discovery + Service Graph Connectors are the enterprise-grade approach. For SaaS, App Portfolio Management (APM) closes the gap. For cloud, ITOM Cloud Discovery normalises across AWS, Azure, and GCP into the CMDB.


## Apply

- Enumerate your four discovery surfaces and identify the one with the weakest coverage today.

## Done When

- You know exactly which surface is producing the biggest blind spot in your inventory.

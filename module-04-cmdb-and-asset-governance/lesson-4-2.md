## Read

The Configuration Management Database (CMDB) is the enduring record of what the organisation owns and how it fits together. The Common Service Data Model (CSDM) is the ServiceNow-endorsed schema that gives the CMDB its shape: from foundational (Companies, Locations), through design (Business Applications, Services), build (Application Services, Software Instances), and manage (Configuration Items, IPs, hostnames).

For a GRC professional the CMDB is not an IT-ops artefact — it is the substrate every risk assessment sits on. A risk that cannot point at a CI, a Service, or a Business Application is a risk that cannot be aggregated, reported, or defended.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

Invest in CMDB Health dashboards (Completeness, Correctness, Compliance) before you invest in any advanced IRM analytics. IRM analytics built on a broken CMDB produce false confidence.


## Apply

- Pull your organisation's CMDB Health scores. If they are below 80% on any dimension, that is your prerequisite to further IRM investment.

## Done When

- You can articulate the CSDM layers and place your top risks against Business Applications.

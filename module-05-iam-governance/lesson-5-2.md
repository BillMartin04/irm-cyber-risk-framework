## Read

Privileged Access Management (PAM) governs the identities that can materially change your infrastructure: domain admins, root accounts, cloud administrators, service accounts with production access. The two design questions are: where are these credentials stored (vaulting), and how are their sessions monitored (session recording). Every regulator now asks both.



## Apply

- Enumerate your top-tier privileged accounts and confirm each is vaulted with session recording enabled.

## Done When

- 100% of your named privileged accounts are vaulted and monitored.

## Read

The Joiner / Mover / Leaver (JML) workflow is where IAM meets HR. Joiners must be provisioned with the correct role bundle on day one. Movers must have their previous entitlements revoked when they change roles. Leavers must lose access within a documented SLA — typically same-day for privileged access and next-day for standard access.

The mover step is the one most commonly broken. Employees accumulate entitlements as they move between roles, producing 'privilege creep' that Access Reviews then have to clean up.

## ServiceNow Implementation Notes

ServiceNow HR Service Delivery + IT Service Management give you the JML orchestration. IRM Assessments ride on top to certify that the workflow is producing the expected outcomes.


## Apply

- Time your leaver access-revocation SLA end-to-end for a recent departure.

## Done When

- Your JML process meets its documented SLA in >95% of cases.

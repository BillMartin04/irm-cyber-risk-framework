## Read

Multi-Factor Authentication is now table stakes, but MFA exemptions are the softest attack surface in most organisations. Every exemption — for a legacy application, a shared account, or an executive who resisted enrolment — must be time-limited, compensating-controlled, and reviewed.



## Apply

- Pull the list of MFA-exempt accounts. If it is not zero, each one needs an exception record with expiry.

## Done When

- Your MFA exemption register has expiry dates on every entry.

## Read

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 defines the requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS). The 2022 revision restructured Annex A into 93 controls across four themes — Organizational, People, Physical, and Technological — and introduced 11 net-new controls covering topics like threat intelligence, cloud security, and secure coding. The ISMS itself — clauses 4 through 10 — is what a certification body actually audits.

Module 18 goes deep on Lead Implementer craft. Here in Module 3 the goal is to place ISO 27001 correctly on your framework map: it is the certification-grade backbone that most enterprise customers will ask for when procuring services.



## Apply

- Locate the four Annex A themes and match five of your existing controls to specific Annex A control numbers.

## Done When

- You can explain the difference between the ISMS clauses and Annex A controls.

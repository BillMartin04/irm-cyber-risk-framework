## Read

NIST CSF 2.0 reorganises the framework around six functions: **Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover**. The elevation of Govern to a peer function is the most important change since the framework was first published. It formalises what mature security programs already do — treat governance as an operating function rather than a background policy exercise.

For a GRC professional, CSF 2.0 is the framework of first resort when a client or executive asks 'what should our cyber program look like'. It is broad enough to cover the whole program, specific enough to trace to controls, and universally recognised.

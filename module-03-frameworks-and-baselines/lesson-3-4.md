## Read

Industry baselines apply on top of framework foundations. PCI DSS v4.0 governs cardholder data environments. HIPAA Security Rule governs Protected Health Information in the US healthcare sector. SOC 2 Type II is the trust-services attestation that SaaS buyers use as procurement gate.

The mature GRC professional never picks between frameworks — they layer them. NIST CSF as backbone, ISO 27001 as management system, then the specific baseline required by the industry (PCI, HIPAA) or the customer (SOC 2).



## Apply

- Identify which industry baseline actually gates your organisation's revenue. Prioritise it.

## Done When

- You can name which baseline your customers will ask for evidence of.

## The three-part rhythm — Watch, Read, Apply

Every lesson is built for a 30 to 60 minute focused session:

1. **Watch** the short video (when published) for the concept in motion.
2. **Read** the written page for the design decisions, platform constructs, and traps.
3. **Apply** the exercise in your own environment or on a scenario. Every lesson has a **Done When** check.

## Suggested pace

- **Beginner** — one lesson per weekday, one module per two weeks, full course in ~40 weeks with time for the capstone.
- **Working professional** — one full module per week, 20 weeks end-to-end.
- **Immersive** — one module per day, 20 days plus the capstone.

## Bring your own risk

Every module deliverable is more useful if you complete it against a real risk from your own organisation. Pick one risk in Module 1 and carry it through the course.

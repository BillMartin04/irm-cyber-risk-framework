# Who This Course Is For

## Primary audiences

- **Aspiring and mid-career GRC professionals** — analysts, risk officers, IT auditors, compliance specialists — who want to move from operating a compliance calendar to architecting a defensible enterprise program.
- **ServiceNow architects and consultants** — CTA-track implementers, IRM consultants, presales engineers — who want to master the cyber-risk domain their platform serves, not just the platform itself.
- **CISOs and heads of risk** — leaders who want a shared vocabulary and operating model they can push down through their teams.
- **External advisors and consultants** — solo advisors and boutique firms who want a portable, repeatable methodology and an open-source demo asset (Lumina) they can carry between clients.

## Assumed background

You should already have a working vocabulary in enterprise IT, some exposure to security concepts (least privilege, MFA, incident response), and comfort reading diagrams and policy documents. You do **not** need to be a ServiceNow developer to complete this course.

## Not the right course for you if…

- You are looking for a beginner cyber-security awareness course. Try a foundational security-awareness program first.
- You need a technical, hands-keyboard SOC analyst course. This is a governance-and-architecture course.
- You want a rote CSA / CIS-RC exam-cram. Use the [ServiceNow CSA Course](https://github.com/BillMartin04/servicenow-csa-course) alongside this one for that.
